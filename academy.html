Wendell Coffee · PGA Professional

Decades of nationally recognized instruction.

Georgia PGA Teacher of the Year in 2000 and named among America’s Top 50 instructors by Golf Range Magazine in 2006, Wendell has taught golfers of every age and ability throughout a career spanning more than five decades.
