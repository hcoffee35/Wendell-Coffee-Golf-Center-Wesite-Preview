Ceremony & reception

Flexible spaces for your complete celebration.

The 3,500-square-foot Event Center opened in 2006 and can accommodate large weddings and receptions. The lakeside gazebo offers an outdoor ceremony setting, while panoramic windows, patio areas and indoor options help create a flexible plan for the day.

  • Waterfront gazebo overlooking the seven-acre lake
  • Indoor wedding and reception options
  • Panoramic windows and scenic property views
  • Patio space for overflow and additional seating
  • Sound and video system
  • Tents, staging and dance flooring available
  • Outside catering permitted
  • Non-smoking indoor environment
