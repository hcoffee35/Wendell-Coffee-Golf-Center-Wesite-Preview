Private & Corporate Events

Room to gather, celebrate and connect.

Flexible indoor and outdoor spaces for company meetings, parties, reunions, showers, anniversaries and community events.

Event Center

3,500 square feet with seating for large groups.

Opened in 2006, the Event Center accommodates large receptions, meetings, reunions and celebrations, with capacity depending on room layout. Published ballroom pricing is based on a 220-guest setup, while the larger property can serve groups of up to 500 by combining the Event Center, Clubhouse, patios and grounds.

  • Panoramic windows with scenic views
  • Tables, chairs and room setup included in base rentals
  • Sound and video system
  • Patio and overflow seating options
  • Tents, staging and dance flooring available
  • Outside catering permitted
  • Daylong meetings and weekday rates available by quote
Clubhouse

A relaxed option for gatherings of 100+.

Opened in 1992 and recently remodeled, the Clubhouse includes a large room with a fireplace and bar, two smaller rooms and direct access to the golf shop. It has hosted birthdays, Rotary meetings, wedding receptions, rehearsal dinners, anniversaries, bachelor parties and more.
