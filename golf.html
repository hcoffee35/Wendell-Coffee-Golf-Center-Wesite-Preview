Public golf in Tyrone, Georgia

Play nine. Practice everything.

A scenic par-3 course, one of America’s recognized grass driving ranges and complete short-game facilities.

Nine-hole par 3

A true course experience for every player.

Designed by Rocky Roquemore, the course winds around a seven-acre lake with Bentgrass greens, Zoysia fairways and holes ranging from approximately 130 to 200 yards. It is open from shop opening until sundown, weather permitting.

No tee times are required. Play is first come, first served. Tournaments are scheduled periodically, and private outings may be booked in advance.

Double-ended grass range

Space to practice with purpose.

The range features 63 all-grass hitting stations on one end and 70 on the other, four target greens with bunkers and two fairways for realistic ball-flight practice.

Short-game facilities

Practice where scoring happens.

Two large putting greens, sand traps, a 100-yard pitching area, wedge practice and a nine-hole natural-grass putting course make it possible to work on every scoring club in one visit.
